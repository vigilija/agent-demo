Only approved AI tools may be used for company work. Client data, personal data and source code must never be entered into public AI chatbots.

Output from AI tools must always be reviewed by a person before it is shared with clients. The employee who shares the output remains responsible for its accuracy.

New AI use cases must be registered with the IT risk team, who assess them against the EU AI Act risk categories before they are put into production.
