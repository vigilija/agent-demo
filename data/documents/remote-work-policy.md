Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are office days for all teams, so that people can collaborate in person.

Remote work from abroad is allowed for a maximum of 20 working days per calendar year and must be approved in advance by your manager and HR, because of tax and insurance rules.

The company provides a one-off home office budget of 500 euros for a desk, chair or monitor. Receipts must be submitted through the expense tool within 30 days of purchase.
