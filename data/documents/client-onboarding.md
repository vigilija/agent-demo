Every new client goes through a know-your-customer (KYC) check before any work starts. The account manager collects the chamber of commerce extract, the identity documents of the ultimate beneficial owners, and a signed engagement letter.

The compliance team reviews the KYC file within five working days. High-risk clients, for example those operating in sanctioned countries, require an additional review by the head of compliance.

After approval, the client receives a welcome pack and a kick-off meeting is scheduled within two weeks.
