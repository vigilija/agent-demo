Suspected security incidents, such as phishing emails, lost laptops or data sent to the wrong recipient, must be reported to the security desk within one hour of discovery.

Do not try to investigate the incident yourself and do not delete suspicious emails. Forward phishing emails as an attachment to the security desk.

If personal data may have been leaked, the privacy officer decides within 72 hours whether the incident must be reported to the Dutch Data Protection Authority.
