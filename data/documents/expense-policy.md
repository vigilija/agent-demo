Business travel within the Netherlands should be done by public transport. First-class train tickets are allowed for journeys longer than two hours.

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 150 euros per night in Europe and 200 euros per night outside Europe. Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 60 euros per day.

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days with a receipt. Expenses above 1,000 euros require pre-approval from your manager.
